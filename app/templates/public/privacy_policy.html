Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 (Version 1.0)

1. Information We Collect

ANTI MATRIX collects personal, academic, and identity information solely for processing internship applications, verifying candidate credentials, issuing formal offer/joining letters, evaluating project submissions, and providing tamper-proof certificate verification.

2. Sensitive Document Protection

Identity documents (such as masked Aadhaar or college ID cards) submitted for 3-month professional internships are stored securely on restricted storage volumes. Sensitive personal data is strictly masked in administration views and is never made publicly accessible on certificate verification pages.

3. Password Security Guarantee

ANTI MATRIX strictly operates an isolated, cryptographically salted password system. We will never ask for or store passwords to your Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, or college portal accounts.

4. Public Certificate Verification

Public verification displays only non-sensitive details: Student Name, Program Track, Duration, Completion Dates, and Certificate Status. No phone numbers, emails, or personal identification records are exposed.
